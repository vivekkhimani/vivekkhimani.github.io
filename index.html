Vivek Khimani

Software engineer at Semgrep · San Francisco Bay Area

I got into computer science because I loved mathematics, and it felt like a good place to put it to work. I liked the point where an abstract idea stops being an idea and starts doing something in the real world. I spent a few years working on privacy-preserving machine learning, where that tension was especially clear. The data that could make a model most useful is often the data people have the strongest reason to keep private.

I first came across Semgrep while I was in college, when I used it to grade data structures assignments. I liked the product enough that I eventually joined the company. I got to work with the nicest people, start new products from scratch, and see them make their way into the hands of customers. It was refreshing to step outside of writing code and see how the rest of the company worked: how customers made decisions, how products were sold and supported, and how all the pieces fit together.
