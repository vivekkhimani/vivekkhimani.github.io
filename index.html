Vivek Khimani

Software engineer at Semgrep, on the platforms team. I work on backend systems, and I am most interested in the places where networking, machine learning and security overlap.

Before Semgrep I interned at Meta on Ads ML Infra and at SIG on market data frameworks. I studied computer science at Drexel, with minors in mathematics and data science, and spent a term researching federated learning at HKUST. Outside work I am usually outdoors, running, playing squash, cooking something vegetarian, or losing an argument with a guitar.
