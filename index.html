Vivek Khimani

Software engineer at Semgrep · San Francisco Bay Area

I came to computer science from mathematics, and stayed for the point where the math makes contact with reality. For a few years that meant privacy-preserving machine learning, a phrase that sounds like a contradiction and often is. Health data is the clearest case for it: the records that would train the best models are the ones people are right to keep private.

I used Semgrep before I worked there, to grade data structures assignments. It was the first open-source developer tool I genuinely liked. Joining pre-revenue and watching the company grow has taught me more than anything else I have done. Eventually I want to build something of my own, probably for an industry far from Silicon Valley. Good software is scarce in those places, and one well-made tool can change how an entire trade works.

I have been an athlete my whole life. Squash was the serious one until college; I still play, along with tennis, and I run most days. Sport is also how I learned to learn: take a thing apart, stay with it well past the moment it stops being fun, keep going until the root cause turns up. Running clears my head. Reading takes me somewhere else entirely, usually fiction and memoir, with a soft spot for murder mysteries and for anything built on a real life. I cook, and I am slowly getting better at the guitar.
