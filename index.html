Vivek Khimani

Software engineer at Semgrep · San Francisco Bay Area

Most of what I have worked on lives in the same corner: systems that have to work without a trusted middle, or without pooling what they know. My research was federated and split learning, which is that problem in miniature. Security turned out to rhyme with it more than I expected.

I grew up in Rajkot, studied computer science in Philadelphia, spent a term in Hong Kong and a summer on research in Hyderabad, and live in the Bay Area now. Outside work I run, play racket sports, cook vegetarian food, and read more fiction than I plan to. I taught eight computer science courses as an undergraduate and still think explaining something is the fastest way to find out whether you understand it.
